The Farmer Was Replaced is a lovely idle game about programming a drone in a subset of the Python language to farm progressively more complicated crops. The real fun of the game, however, wasn't unlocked until the very end of the game where you are suddenly granted the ability to use more than one drone concurrently, rendering your by now incredibly complicated single threaded farming algorithms woefully inefficient.

About two thirds of the achievements in this game will occur through natural play, and are omitted from the guide. The remainder will be grouped by resource type.
